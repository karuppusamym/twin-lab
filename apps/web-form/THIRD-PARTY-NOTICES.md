pages listed in asset-manifest.json. The application logic in those responses is reused, not
claimed as original authorship. Vendor copyright/license headers remain intact.

Font licenses and applicable top-level project licenses are included in licenses/.

and a local hosting/health wrapper added. No source service endorsement is implied.
